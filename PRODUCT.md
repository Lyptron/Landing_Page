# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Clients of a digital agency (Lyptron) logging into their project's client portal via an access code. Non-technical decision-makers (founders, marketing leads) checking project status, approving deliverables, reviewing invoices, and communicating with the agency team — usually in short, focused sessions rather than continuous daily use.

## Product Purpose

Give agency clients a single place to see project health, timeline, deliverables, financials, meetings, and team — replacing scattered email/Slack updates with a persistent, always-current workspace. Success is a client who trusts the agency more after using it: informed without needing to ask, reassured by clarity even when a project is behind schedule.

## Brand Personality

Boutique studio, not generic SaaS: warm, precise, handcrafted. Confident and calm — never alarmist, even in "at risk" states. Should feel like the agency's own product, not a white-labeled dashboard template.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS admin templates (interchangeable card grids, gradient-text hero metrics, cookie-cutter icon+heading+text tiles). Cold enterprise dashboards (dense navy/gray, no personality). Anything that reads as a stock admin theme rather than something Lyptron built.

## Design Principles

- Warmth through material, not decoration — the off-white/ink palette and restrained indigo accent already read boutique; premium comes from precision (spacing, motion, typography) not more color or ornament.
- Calm authority in status — health/urgency states (on-track/at-risk/delayed) stay legible without becoming alarming; color is earned, not default.
- One brand voice across marketing site and portal — the portal is often a client's most-used surface, so it should feel as considered as the landing page, not like an afterthought admin panel.
- Every screen answers "where do things stand" before it asks the client to do anything.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text contrast). Reduced-motion alternative for all portal transitions (sidebar active-state spring, sidebar slide-in, loading spinner).
